01 / Program structure

Different roles. Focused work products.

TRACK 1 · 48 HOURS · 8 WEEKS

Policy Analysis & Research

Specialists, Associates and Senior Associates; under 6 years’ experience.

16 sessions × 3 hours. Build evidence-backed research, policy analysis, proposal sections and stakeholder synthesis.

Learning outcome

Use approved AI tools to research, analyse and draft work with source traceability and human review.

TRACK 2 · 42 HOURS · 7 WEEKS

Transformation Roadmap

Managers, Senior Managers and Directors; 6–15 years’ experience.

14 sessions × 3 hours. Build workflow diagnostics, gap analysis, operating models, investment notes and roadmaps.

Learning outcome

Diagnose a transformation problem and develop a justified roadmap with value measures and governance.

04 / Progressive capstone

Start early. Improve at every milestone.

Groups of 5–6. Introduce around Session 2–3. Tracks remain independent.

Track 1 submission package

  • Problem statement and research scope
  • Evidence register with source references
  • Policy analysis and comparison
  • Stakeholder synthesis, where inputs exist
  • Options and supported recommendations
  • Executive research brief
  • Governance and human-review record
  • Presentation and individual contribution record

Suggested case: a client-approved government policy or scheme question.

Final draft: S14 · Refinement: S15 · Defence: S16

Track 2 submission package

  • Transformation charter
  • Current-state diagnostic and gap analysis
  • Opportunity register and prioritisation
  • Target operating model and future workflow
  • Investment note with assumptions
  • Value-realisation plan
  • Risk and governance plan
  • Roadmap, presentation and contribution record

Suggested case: a client-approved government process or service.

Final draft and refinement: S13 · Defence: S14

Responsibilities

Trainer: Prepare, demonstrate, facilitate, review, mentor, grade and report daily highlights.

Program PMO: Calendars, faculty, attendance, learner records, submissions, reporting and escalations.

PwC/client: Participants, protected learning time, tools/labs, cases, mentors and panels.

Learners: Pre-work, attendance, submissions, capstone contributions and feedback responses.

Mentors/panel: Milestone reviews and final assessment.

Reporting cadence

Daily: Topics, labs, outputs, challenges, capstone progress and next activities.

Weekly: Attendance, access, submissions and delivery readiness.

Weekly/fortnightly: Quality, assessments, risks and corrective actions.

At milestones: Sponsor review of outcomes and escalations.

End of track: Completion, learning gain, quality, capstone results and reinforcement needs.

After 30 days: Manager review of workplace adoption.
